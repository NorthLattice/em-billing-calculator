Each subsequent prolonged-service unit requires 15 more qualifying minutes. No partial units or midpoint rounding.

Why two thresholds?

CPT starts the first prolonged unit 15 minutes beyond the base minimum. CMS uses a later threshold: 89 minutes for 99205 and 69 for 99215. The base code still starts at 60 or 40 minutes under either method.

Choose the visit by time

This tool assumes time-based selection of 99205 or 99215. Although base office E/M codes may also be selected by medical decision-making, these prolonged-service calculations require selection by time.

Document qualifying work

Document the total time and services performed. Include qualifying physician or qualified health care professional (QHP) work on the encounter date, such as record review, evaluation, counseling, and documentation. Exclude staff time, separately reported services, travel, and overlapping time counted twice.

Check the payer

Commercial and Medicare Advantage policies may differ. Confirm code acceptance, coverage, unit limits, and edits for the service date. Results are educational, not a payment guarantee. Other settings and code families are outside this tool’s scope.
